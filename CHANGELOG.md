# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The Drowned City: the route is now shown on the second level, Atal'Byzal, too (needs Forever Dungeon Routes 1.2.7 or newer).

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Shows the active Forever Dungeon Routes route on the DungeonJournal dungeon maps: paths with direction arrows, numbered boss, rare and optional stops, notes and level changes.
- The route colors follow the distance along the route, like in Forever Dungeon Routes.
- Route menu and show/hide button on the map, `/fdjr` to toggle the route.
- Works on the journal's own map and on the world map inside a dungeon.
- RPG Mode of DungeonJournal is respected: undiscovered bosses stay "???".
